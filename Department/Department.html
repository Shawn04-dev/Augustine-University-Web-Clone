MEET THE DEAN

DEAN OF THE FACULTY OF COMPUTING

Dr. Dada Olaniyi Aborisade is a distinguished scholar and lecturer in the field of computer science and information systems in Nigeria. He holds a Bachelor of Science (Bsc.) Degree in Mathematical Sciences (Computer Science option) from the University of Agriculture, Abeokuta, Nigeria, and a Master of Science (MSc) Degree from the University of Ibadan, Nigeria.

He completed his PhD in Computer Science at the University of Ibadan, Nigeria, in 2017. Dr. Aborisade has over ten years of teaching and research experience, with a total of twenty publications in both local and international conferences and journals. His research interests include Cloud Information Security, Digital Forensics, and Systems Survivability. He has also contributed to the AI4PEP initiative, focusing on digital transformation and cybersecurity readiness in public and health sectors.
